Settings

Settings belong to this extension only. By default the API key is kept in Thunderbird session storage and cleared on restart. If you choose to remember it, it is stored unencrypted in your Thunderbird profile. Either way it is used only with the endpoint it was saved for. Saving settings does not send a provider request.

Privacy and data flow

When you trigger generation, Compose Assist sends your text above the quote, quoted context (including quoted history), subject, sender and recipient addresses, date, and attachment filenames to the configured HTTPS provider. Attachment contents are not sent. Encrypted originals are rejected without decryption, but pasted text cannot be identified as previously encrypted.

When you trigger a suggestion, Move Suggest sends the selected message's subject, author, recipients, CC, date, body text and attachment filenames, together with the eligible folder names, to the configured HTTPS provider. Attachment contents are not sent, and encrypted messages are rejected without decryption.

Requests set store: false, but your provider's processing and retention policies still apply. Email content leaves your computer; only use a provider you trust with it. Compose Assist never sends email automatically, and Move Suggest only moves a message when you confirm. Review generated replies before sending.
